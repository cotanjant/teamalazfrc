Our Mission
Engineering with Purpose. Inspiring Through Innovation.
Our mission is to empower students through hands-on engineering, scientific exploration, and
collaborative problem-solving. By participating in the FIRST Robotics Competition, we aim to develop
innovative robotic solutions while building technical expertise, leadership, and teamwork. We strive to
make STEM education more accessible, share our knowledge with our community, and inspire the
next generation to explore the possibilities of science and technology.
Our Vision
Shaping Tomorrow Through Engineering and Innovation.
Our vision is to become a team recognized for engineering excellence, creative thinking, and
meaningful community impact. We aspire to push the boundaries of robotics, establish lasting
partnerships, and contribute to a future where technology serves humanity. Through continuous
learning, sustainable growth, and a commitment to the values of FIRST, we aim to create an impact
that extends far beyond the competition field.

Outreach
Inspiring Minds. Empowering Communities.
At ALAZ, we believe that the impact of engineering extends far beyond the competition field. We work
to promote STEM education, introduce young people to science and technology, and develop
initiatives that address community needs. Through knowledge sharing, volunteering, and

makes a meaningful difference in the world around us.
